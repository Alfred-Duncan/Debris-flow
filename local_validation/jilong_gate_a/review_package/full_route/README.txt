This package is for external human visual review of the complete S0-S4 corridor.

Reference ordering only: official upstream vector, the pre-existing 607.99 m imagery-supported gap trace, and the pre-existing downstream human-review correction to S4. It is not a new candidate route or a Gate A decision.

The upstream anomaly is marked as a known review area. Although a human decision exists in prior materials, no upstream correction line is digitized here because this package must not invent one.

Review every tile without treating this package's numerical context as a classification. For every tile, assign one of:
A - official/reference geometry visually consistent
B - local correction required, route visually clear
C - ambiguous, higher-resolution/additional evidence required

Tile panels share a tile extent. The CSV records imagery/DEM footprint coverage and raw numerical adverse-rise context sampled at about 12.5 m. These metrics do not approve or reject a tile.

Tiles: 16. Chainage is a review-order coordinate from S0, not a final canonical-corridor measurement.
