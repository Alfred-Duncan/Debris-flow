# Downstream geometry correction QA

## Decision

`HUMAN_IMAGE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; no corrected local channel has been accepted.

## Rejected official segment

The P0--P2 official segment is 1878.66 m long. It leaves the visible valley/channel and has a maximum 200 m adverse rise over a 250 m window in the native 12.5 m DEM. Its maximum valid one-step local rise is 25 m. The 8 m DEM has valid coverage over 100.0% of sampled locations and independently supports the anomalous uphill terrain trend where it is valid.

## Why no replacement is published

The available 0.5 m pre-event DOM and 3 m post-event image both show valley/linear features, but they do not provide a uniquely traceable continuous P0-to-S4 channel axis at the required confidence. Drawing one would turn an unresolved visual interpretation into false corridor geometry. Therefore candidate length, offsets, and corrected-trace elevation comparisons are intentionally `null`, not inferred.

## Required human decision

Review figures 15--18 and approve a continuous image-supported channel trace, or supply a mapped event-channel product. No DEM was modified and no simulation was run.
