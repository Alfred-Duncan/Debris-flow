# RiskTemporal B10

RiskTemporal B10 combines a frozen Global Operator with Support-Risk ROI allocation, spatial de-redundancy, one-step Temporal Refresh, a frozen Local Corrector, wet-support and momentum-state guards, physical projection, and autoregressive rollout.

The B10 allocation selects at most 19 of 256 logical patches per step. Inference is deterministic and has no ground-truth access.
